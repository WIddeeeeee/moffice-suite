Formats MOffice supports

Everything runs in your browser. No uploads, no account, no server — your files stay on your device.

📄 MWord

Open: .txt, .md, .html
Save: .txt, .md, .html, .pdf (via Print)
Note: .docx opens in Word/Google Docs from the .html export — rename-free, standards-based.

📊 MSheets

Open: .csv, .tsv, .json
Save: .csv, .tsv, .json
Note: CSV opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

🎬 MVideo

Open: .mp4, .webm, .mov, .mkv, .avi*
Save: .webm (recorded export)
Note: *playback depends on your browser's codecs — Chrome/Edge play MP4 + WebM, Safari plays MP4 + MOV.

🎵 Audio

Open: .mp3, .wav, .ogg, .m4a
Stock: 5 built-in royalty-free loops, generated in-browser, plus sound effects in code.

How to use it

  1. Open index.html — or host this folder on any static host / GitHub Pages.
  2. Pick MWord, MSheets or MVideo from the top.
  3. Save Project downloads one JSON with your document, sheet and video settings.
